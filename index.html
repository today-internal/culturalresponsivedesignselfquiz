About you

Map your capability across seven building blocks. Do it once, use it across projects, and redo it when your capability changes. It takes 20 to 30 minutes.

Instructions:

  1. Find a quiet space. Give yourself 20 to 30 minutes without interruptions.
  2. Use this for self reflection. There are no right answers. Rate where you are today, not where you want to be.
  3. Practise informed consent. Only share what you’re comfortable sharing. Every details box is optional, and you can leave it blank.
  4. Tell us about you. Add your name or nickname, your craft and a symbol that represents you.
  5. Map your capability. For each of the seven building blocks, move the sliders to where you sit between low and high on education, expertise, relationships, lived experience and shared identity. Some measures are greyed out where they don’t apply.
  6. Download your profile. Save it as a PDF or PNG. Nothing is stored, so redo the quiz whenever your capability changes.
  7. Share only with your consent. Anything you share in a project or studio context is only ever with your explicit consent.
Nothing you enter is saved or sent anywhere. Your answers stay in this browser tab, and your profile is generated on your device.
